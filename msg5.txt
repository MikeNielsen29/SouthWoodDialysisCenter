- New "About hosting" section under Care & Maintenance.

On the hosting wording, which was the reason for this change:
- Hosting is already inside the ₱4,500/mo line, and no infrastructure
  vendor is named anywhere in the client-facing documents. Verified:
  "Vercel" does not appear in any generated HTML.
- What the new section does is say plainly that the site runs on managed
  infrastructure that MIPORO chooses and pays for, that the client is not
  billed for bandwidth, and that they get a full data export if they
  leave. Moving hosts is described as a project, not a button, with the
  first migration free within the first year.

That wording was chosen on purpose. Naming no vendor is fine. Leaving the
client to discover the hosting arrangement is not — a brokerage that
believes it owns everything and then finds the site depends on one
person's cloud account has a bad surprise waiting. The section keeps the
vendor private and removes the ambiguity.
