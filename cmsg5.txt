docs(roadmap): record real phase status instead of an empty checklist

ROADMAP.md still opened with a "Universal Definition of Done" where
every box was unticked, which read as "nothing is done" — the same
problem TASKS.md had. It now carries the verified state:

- a status block at the top (live URL, build/test counts, Content DB,
  admin panels) so a new reader or a new client sees where the project
  actually is in ten seconds
- each of the 17 phases marked ✅ / 🟡 / ⏸️ at its own heading
- the DoD checklist marked against evidence rather than intention:
  0/32 horizontal overflow at 360px, but 768/1024/1440 unverified;
  Lighthouse never run; axe-core never run; E2E registered but not
  executed; 9 listing images still 404

The theme is the same one that ran through today's work: a ticked box
means nothing until something verified it, and three of today's
"complete" phases were ticked-open by accident. Where a box stays open
it now says which check is missing and why — network, a client asset,
or an account the owner holds — so nobody re-audits from scratch.

Remaining work is no longer coding. It is photos and listings (client),
the domain (owner), DPO/legal sign-off (post-sale), and running the two
verifications this machine could not finish.
