**Security headers** — `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `X-Content-Type-Options`,
`Referrer-Policy`, `Permissions-Policy` (geolocation explicitly off; a
property listing page has no use for it and it leaks a visitor's exact
position), COOP, and a full CSP, all in `next.config.ts` so a fork
inherits them instead of silently losing them.

⚠️ The CSP is **enforced and unverified against a real page load**. It
keeps `'unsafe-inline'` for script-src — which is what actually breaks a
strict policy here, since Next bootstraps inline and the JSON-LD blocks
are inline — and there is no `'unsafe-eval'`. But if the site renders
blank after deploy, that header is the cause and the fix is documented in
the file and in TASKS.md. HSTS is deliberately left commented out: sending
it while the domain is still parked locks browsers out of it.
